Shadow values from the v0 models: each figure is the expected gross return per ¥1 at the final price. No real money is involved, and nothing on this page is a bet.

Odds now, and how they moved since the first morning capture and since 20 minutes before post. + odds lengthened, - odds shortened, "-" when there is nothing to compare. Value: for Win the mean with its 5–95% band in brackets; for Place and Wide the value at the low and at the high end of the odds range. "Read at" is the minutes before post the value was read at (for example @T-5). One race at a time; rows run favourite first by the current odds, as on the board (Exacta, Trio and Trifecta by Model p, below), never by value. Times are Japan time.

Odds ladder: T-20, T-10, T-5, T-2 and Final are the odds at 20, 10, 5 and 2 minutes before post and the final price (Place and Wide: the low figure). A checkpoint already passed shows the odds captured then, higher or lower than at T-20. A checkpoint still ahead shows ≈x.x: a forecast of the crowd's drift from the v0 nowcast, from the odds now. It is not a value and not a signal to bet. "-" when there is no figure.

Every race shows each bet type: Win, Place, Quinella, Wide, Exacta, Trio, Trifecta and Bracket quinella. Model p is the v0 models' chance of the combination, from the calibrated Win model and the ordering model, on the board as it stands now. Exacta, Trio and Trifecta list the 15 combinations with the highest Model p, highest first, with their odds from the latest capture ("-" when it has none); the other bet types list every combination, favourite first. Quinella, Bracket quinella, Exacta, Trio and Trifecta have no price forecast: their value is Model p × the odds now, the gross return per ¥1 if the odds stood. The capture age says how old those odds are.
