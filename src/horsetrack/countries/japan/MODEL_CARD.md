### C1: final-price nowcast (#85; leaderboard input)

- C1 structural nowcast (#85, `models/nowcast.py`): a forecast of each pool's final shares from the board at t = post − τ, read from rows in `logger.compact.SCHEMA` (a row counts from its `fetched_utc`, its `snapshot_utc` where it has none, prices and pool totals alike; each combination takes its latest row at or before t). Shares are money over the pool: per-combination money when the rows carry it, else 1/O normalised for Win and, for the stake-back-share pools, the figures read by JRA's rules: Place by the stake-back-share rule (`core.pricing.stake_back_share_dividend`), a Place board with high figures by least squares over both figures (`place_money_fit`, #410), else the low figures inverted in closed form; a Place board's places paid P come from the field at the start of sales, the runners priced on the race's earliest Place board, so a runner scratched after sales open leaves P as it was (#410). A joint fit whose figures above the floor hold some runners' money only as a sum (an SVD rank test of its equations) keeps its minimum-norm shares and is flagged `undetermined` by `place_shares`; the rank is computed only where the read is named (`place_shares`, each case's closing board), not in `pool_shares`, `race_view` or the draws. Wide is read by JRA's pair rule (the pair {a,b} wins with {a,x} and {b,x} for one third runner x, its low figure with max over x of m_ax + m_bx, its high figure with the min, each winning pair paid by the same stake-back-share identity of `core.pricing`, (1 − takeout)(1 + D / (3 m)) with D the losers' money; `wide_money`, iterated to the rule's fixed point). Each pool is timed by its own rows' post time, and a runner scratched in the Win or Place rows takes its Wide pairs off the board from the reporting row's `fetched_utc` on (its `snapshot_utc` where it has none). The model is s_final = φ_t s_t + (1 − φ_t) s_late. φ_t = V_t / V_final comes from a pool-growth profile, ln(V_final / V_m) = h(m) exp(θ·x): h is piecewise linear in the board's minutes to post m, and x holds venue, race number and grade (ridge). The late composition is logistic-normal, s_late = softmax(ln s_t + β·f + σ ε). Its features f are the share velocities over [t−5, t] and [t−15, t] and ln of the favourite rank. β comes from least squares within race on the observed late-money ratio. σ, and the random walk's σ, each maximise the training log score of the final ln-shares. There is one (β, σ) cell per pool and τ of the D2 grid. Draws are share vectors with shares ≥ φ_t s_t, so their spread scales with 1 − φ_t, and V_final = V_t / φ_t. The random-walk baseline is softmax(ln s_t + σ_rw ε). The model is pool-generic and fits Wide wherever Wide paths exist. `Nowcast.draws(view, pool=, n=, seed=)` draws its own shocks; `Nowcast.draws_from_shocks(view, pool=, shocks=)`, the interface the decision layer calls, takes per-runner shocks (a Wide pair's (ε_a + ε_b)/√2) so every pool is drawn from one set. `ShadowNowcast` puts a fit behind #116's draws interface, reading a Place board's high figures as `race_view` does (Wide figures printed by the pair rule), with the race's grade taken from the stored race card's `race_class` ("grade=none" without one), and a pointer file naming a `nowcast_c1` fit switches the shadow values to it (`models/v0/loader.py`). `current.json` still names #116's drift. Fits refuse holdout days, and the D9 fold fits (`fit_folds`) see only their training days. A late scratch follows the owner's #69 rule: a combination priced at t and not at the close is removed, every forecaster's drawn shares are renormalised over the combinations priced at the close, and the case is scored; the fits train on the cases whose board at t is the final board. It feeds the leaderboard.
- Its run (`studies/nowcast.py`, `uv run horsetrack japan nowcast-study`, `docs/experiments/nowcast-japan.md`) trains on the JRA-VAN time-series paths in the store, Win and Place, and on our own compacted logged development days as they accrue (#411: rows with a post time only; a JRA-VAN path with a post time wins its race and pool, so our logs add Wide; a logged path counts only with a confirmed final capture, and one whose capture stopped before the close is counted, not used), on race days from 2014-06-07 (D10) on D9's folds. It scores the nowcast against the random walk and against #116's drift (D8's price baseline, fitted on the same boards) per pool and τ, by log score and CRPS of the final ln-shares with race-day and meeting bootstrap bounds, PIT and 50%/90% coverage. JRA-VAN is a licensed source, so every figure is withheld (D21). In v2 only Win was sound: its Place inversion used a wrong rule. #410's v3 (family `japan-c1-nowcast-final-shares-v3`) reads Place by JRA's rule. v5 (`japan-c1-nowcast-final-shares-v5`, spec sha256 `a07a5be8…`, git `0f0df42`) measures that rule on the closing boards of the scored Place cases, read as scored, against the final Place votes, and keeps coverage and agreement apart: a determined read stands as a result only when its shares are within a stated tolerance on at least `PLACE_MIN_WITHIN_TOLERANCE` of its validated boards. Its measurement is record `86795d4a880bbd20`; whether each read stands as a result, and so which Place cases the gains hold, is withheld (D21), see the private report. The figures are in the private report `nowcast-japan-a07a5be8273d.md`. v4's verdict (record `8810ca538e68f8e4`) took coverage for agreement and v3's measurement validated other boards; both are superseded. The JRA-VAN series has no Wide pool, so Wide comes from our logs alone: it is fitted and scored where the folds' training days hold Wide paths with post times (compaction now takes post times from the race card where the sidecar has none), and otherwise the report states why it is not, naming each missing input (a confirmed final capture, a board before the post); a run with no scored case still renders its report. The two logged Wide days of #85's run carried no post times, so that run did not fit Wide.

### C2: dynamic rating (#91; leaderboard input)

- C2 dynamic rating (#91, `models/rating.py`): each horse's ability is a Gaussian random walk, N(prior_mean, prior_var) at its debut, with drift_var_per_day times the days off added between races and spell_var added after more than spell_days off. A race updates every runner by Weng and Lin's Bradley-Terry full-pair rule (performance variance perf_var, a dead heat at 1/2), in race order, so the state before a race (mu_before, sigma_before = σ_h(t)) reads earlier races only; no smoothed (TTT) value is used. `rating_member` gives the within-race log-probability softmax((mu_before − race mean) / sqrt(scale² + sigma_before²)). It feeds the leaderboard. Its run (`studies/rating.py`, `docs/experiments/rating-japan.md`) reads only placings from the JRA results store, picks drift and scale per D9 fold by inner-fold log loss (drift 0, scale 1.5 in every fold), and exports mu_before, sigma_before and the member's logp for every priced starter at each selected pair, with the fold each row is out of fold for (`oof_fold`), under the data dir for the meld (#98). On development folds the member correlates 0.72 with ln q_final, has pseudo-R² 0.12 against the market's 0.26, and takes a negative stage-2 weight beside ln q (α −0.045, meeting-clustered bounds −0.064 and −0.026).

### C2: top-3 Plackett-Luce ability with pooled jockey, trainer and sire (#92; leaderboard input)

- C2 ability (#92, `models/ability.py`): the finishing order's top 3 as three successive choices, stage s a softmax of u over the runners not placed before s, weighted by discounts (1, w2, w3); runners placed 4th or later, and starters without a placing, stay in every choice set and are never chosen (positions 4+ unused, eased runners). A dead heat counts each co-placed runner at 1/m in the shared stage; at k = 1 the likelihood is the conditional logit. u = β · rating + a jockey, a trainer and a sire effect, each effect N(0, τ²) with τ² by empirical Bayes (MacKay's evidence fixed point), fitted as a penalised MAP by Newton's method. Each runner gets u, its Laplace standard error σ_i (an unseen jockey, trainer or sire at effect 0 with its prior variance) and p_ability = softmax(u) within the race. It feeds the leaderboard.
- Its run (`studies/ability.py`, `uv run horsetrack japan ability-study`, `docs/experiments/ability-japan.md`) follows the owner's #92 design (2026-11-26): the rating is #91's member at the pair #91's rule picks per fold; the sire is the JRA-VAN pedigree's; level 1 trains on results only from 2010 and is out of fold from 2015-Q1 on D9's quarters, with the discounts and the rating pair re-selected per fold on inner folds from 2011-Q1; level 2 is #87's stage-2 meta at the final Win price against D4's calibrated market, and the member's C4 marginal is its stage-2 meta against the market-only stage-2 meta (u = β · ln q on the same rows and folds). E33 compares it with the same model trained on the winner only, E34 with #129's as-of connection rates (race days in [day − 365, day − 1]) in place of the pooled effects, on races with a runner whose jockey, trainer or sire has fewer than 30 prior starts. The sire is JRA-VAN data, so every figure is withheld (D21); the per-runner export (u, σ_i, p_ability) is under the data dir for the meld (#98), named by the spec and the data builds.

### C2: interactions on the ability model: jockey, trainer, track (#93; ranked, not adopted)

- C2 interactions (#93, `models/interactions.py`): blocks added to #92's `AbilityModel`, each with its own N(0, τ²) prior and τ² by empirical Bayes (the same MacKay fixed point). A variance component gives each cell (e.g. horse × jockey, trainer × venue) one shrunk effect; the structured aptitude gives each horse a_h + b_h·z(d) + c_h·z(d)² + e_h·1{dirt} (z the distance standardised over the training rows, a prior per coefficient); a low-rank block is the rank-one U_r·W_c with W centred at unit scale, so a column a row never ran at is predicted from the factor (E36's east/west term, ⟨U_h, W_track⟩). Blocks of more than 5,000 parameters (horse × jockey, the per-horse aptitude on the full store) take block-Jacobi steps: the MAP is exact, their posterior variances block-diagonal. τ's bounds are the profile of the Laplace evidence over the block's prior scale, each one-sided 95%. `cell_sizes` counts a cell's runs in strictly earlier races; `n_half(τ, I)` = 1/(I·τ²); `drop_aliased` drops a block whose cells are one of its columns' levels (horse × trainer where every horse keeps one trainer); `transfer_step` marks the runs after a stable change of trainer (the new trainer kept for 2 consecutive runs).
- Its run (`studies/interactions.py`, `uv run horsetrack japan interaction-study`, `docs/experiments/interactions-japan.md`) reads the JRA results store only, so its figures are public. The base is #92's model without the sire (rating plus pooled jockey and trainer, discounts fixed at (1, 0.5, 0.25)); the members add horse × jockey (E35's negative control), trainer × track as a variance component plus the one-factor term (E36), the horse aptitude (structured plus ⟨U_h, W_track⟩), the trainer debut prior (a debut indicator and a trainer × debut component) and the transfer step. Horse × trainer is not fitted (aliased), recorded with its cell counts. Level 1 is out of fold from 2015-Q1 on D9's quarters; level 2 is #87's stage-2 meta at the final Win price, each block member paired against the base's stage-2 meta, with its tail return by claim band, settled at the store's recorded Win dividend per race and horse number. The level-1 fits are checkpointed with each member-fold's fitting revision, which every record names. A horse × jockey estimate is predictive, never causal (booking endogeneity, 05 §4.2), and never a bet trigger.

### C2: lineage prior (#95; E37 run by #538, not adopted)

- C2 lineage (#95, `models/lineage.py`): `relationship_matrix` is the additive relationship A among a set of horses by the tabular method over their ancestors only (inbreeding counted, a founder or an unknown parent at 0). `LineagePrior` fits, on the runs strictly before a time t, the same discounted top-3 Plackett-Luce likelihood as #92 with u = g(sire, surface, band) + ¼ · g(damsire, surface, band), where g(s, cell) = g_sire[s] + d[s, cell], g_sire ~ N(0, τ_sire²) with τ_sire² by empirical Bayes and d ~ N(0, τ_cell²) at the caller's τ_cell, as a penalised MAP. One table serves the sire and the damsire roles. Only stallions with a progeny run before t, and their cells (`sire_cells`), have parameters: an unseen cell takes g_sire, and an unseen or unknown stallion takes 0. The debut prior mean of a horse's ability in a cell is the trainer's effect plus that lineage term (`prior_mean`, `prior_means`); `cell_effects_` is the fitted table per stallion × surface × band, the prior #93's aptitudes can start from. Surfaces and distance bands are the caller's labels, and the bands' cut points belong to E37's frozen spec. The model has no per-horse effect.
- E37's scoring (`studies/lineage.py`): `run_segments` flags each run as debut, first time on the surface or first time in the distance band from that horse's earlier runs only, and `segment_delta_ll` gives each segment's paired per-race winner ΔLL with D7's meeting bounds, standalone (the ability member with the prior against it without) and at the final price (the stage-2 metas).
- E37's run (#538, `studies/lineage.py`, `uv run horsetrack japan lineage-study`, `docs/experiments/lineage-japan.md`; family `japan-c2-lineage-e37-netkeiba-v3`, superseding v2, whose lineage members failed on folds whose training rows had no nonzero feature, and v1, whose run stopped on a 3-sire block in 2014): the pedigree is netkeiba's `horse_pedigree` keyed on the normalised sire, dam and damsire names (`netkeiba_pedigree`, which refuses a table carrying a UKC/JRDB column), so the family reads no licensed source and its figures are public. The base member is #92's pooled member with the netkeiba sire name in place of the JRA-VAN sire (the same frame, D9 quarters, rating pair and discount selection); a fit carries sire effects only when its training rows hold at least 50 netkeiba sires, and priors are fitted only once 50 stallions have a progeny run (both from 2016-10-01). One `LineagePrior` is fitted per D9 quarter on the runs before 00:00 UTC of its first day, and `lineage_feature` gives each run that quarter's prior mean at its horse, surface (turf, dirt) and #438's distance band (up to 1400, 1800, 2200 m, longer) when the horse has fewer than 30 prior starts (debut included), 0 otherwise: training and test rows alike read only earlier quarters. Each lineage member is the base member at the fold's selection plus that unpenalised feature (a fold whose training rows have the feature 0 throughout takes the base member's fit), for τ_cell ∈ {0.1, 0.3} × damsire weight ∈ {0, ¼}. Level 2 is #92's stage-2 meta and market-only meta at the final Win price, scored on the D9 folds of the netkeiba coverage window (every quarter from the first with at least 80% of its starters covered). Each variant × segment (debut, first surface, first distance, all) is a ranked member row; none is adopted. On the 22 folds from 2021-Q3 every lineage member beats the base member alone on every segment (lower bounds above 0, largest on debuts), but at the final price the stage-2 meta with it gains nothing over the one without (ledger, E37 #538). A JRA-VAN-pedigree variant over the full window is not run (a follow-up).

### C3: race-level finish speed (#96, scored by #305; leaderboard input)

C3 forecasts a race's top-3 time at the decision time.
- **Code:** the model is `countries/japan/models/speed.py` (#96). The run is `countries/japan/studies/speed_run.py` (#305).
- **Data:** the store's JRA races and runners (netkeiba results), column-scoped. No JRDB or JRA-VAN input is read.
  Holdout days are dropped before anything else, and jump races are excluded.
- **Target:** `top3_time_s`, the mean finish time of the runners placed 1–3 (owner, #96).
  - It is read per runner: each runner placed 1 or 2 counts once, and the runners placed 3rd share one slot at their
    mean.
  - A race with no 3rd-placed runner uses the runners placed 1–2. There are 110 such races.
- **Model:** a linear mixed model, `top3_time = par(cell) + v(day, track, surface) + ε`.
  - The cell is track × surface × distance × layout × rail × class. Class is a category with no scale imposed (the
    class scale is the owner's), and a missing key is the level `unknown`.
  - The day effect v is a random effect, N(0, τ²). ε is Student-t (5 df in #305's primary run, df not signed) or
    normal.
  - The model is fitted by maximum likelihood (conditional maximisation: Student-t reweighting, then σ² and τ²
    profiled with v integrated out) on each fold's training races only.
- **Prediction at t:** E[top-3 time] = par + the posterior mean of the day's v.
  - The posterior uses the residuals, against the frozen par, of the same (day, track, surface) races whose result is
    available strictly before t.
  - A result is available at post_utc + 30 min, the owner's same-day results rule (#80, #96). The as-of views carry
    the same rule for the finish columns of races and runners since #305 (`config/availability.yaml`); the study
    applies it itself.
  - With no result by t, v is its prior, so the forecast is the par. No variant prior is injected yet; the cushion and
    moisture prior is E29 (#90, #230).
- **Evaluation:** #70's quarterly expanding walk-forward folds (D9). The decision time is T-2 (D2). The metric is the
  time RMSE against the par-only baseline on the same races, with meeting-clustered paired one-sided 95% bounds (D7).
- **Result (#305):** RMSE 1.009 s against the par's 1.242 s, a gain of +0.233 s (lower bound +0.224). That is 37,047
  development races, 2015-Q4 to 2026-11-23 ([docs/experiments/speed-japan.md](../../../../docs/experiments/speed-japan.md)).
  The gain is 0 on the 26% of races with no same-day result by t.
- **Limitations:**
  - Rail is unknown on every race in the store and layout on 88%, so the 'course' part of the owner's request is not
    resolved yet (#90, #329). The run predates #253, which now fills `rail_setting` in the store build from the
    cached JRA going pages; historical rail still needs #90, and the rerun by rail and layout is #329.
  - The 30-minute publication rule is an assumption in the ledger.
  - The race-level time test is not a test at the final price; E39 (#347, below) is.

### C3: horse-level finish speed and p_speed (#97, scored at the final price by #347; leaderboard input)

C3's horse layer turns each runner's expected finish time at t into a win probability, p_speed.
- **Code:** the model is `countries/japan/models/speed_horse.py` (#97) on #96's `SpeedModel`, with the runs frame
  from `countries/japan/models/speed_runs.py` (#346). The E39 study is `countries/japan/studies/p_speed.py` (#347).
- **Data:** the store's JRA races and runners (netkeiba results) from 2010, column-scoped; holdout days dropped first;
  jump races excluded. Prices only from 2014-06-07 (D10). No JRDB or JRA-VAN input is read.
- **Model:** finish_time = baseline + θ_i(t) + b[style] · pace_r + w · (weight − w_ref) + ε.
  - The baseline is #96's expected top-3 time of the run's race as of its off (a fit day's in-sample variant once
    that day is fully resulted, the owner on #97, Q3 (b)). pace_r is the field's mean early position from earlier
    races (#129; the owner on #97, Q2 (c)).
  - θ_i is a random walk per horse (variance drift_var_per_day per day) from a population prior, forward-filtered by
    Kalman; ε is normal or Student-t. The global terms are maximum likelihood on each fold's training runs.
  - p_speed: Plackett-Luce, softmax(−μ / s) with s = 1 / the conditional-logit coefficient on −μ, or Henery,
    P(fastest) of independent N(μ, s²) times with s its own ML. Fold k's s comes from earlier folds' predictions.
- **Prediction at t:** each test race at the off, from every run and race-level result published strictly before it
  (results at post + 30 min). Per #70 fold, `SpeedModel` is fitted on the training races and observes the test races;
  `HorseSpeed` is fitted on the training runs and observes the test runs in one batch.
- **Evaluation (E39):** standalone, p_speed's winner log loss against D4's calibrated market out of fold; marginal,
  p_speed as a member of #87's stage-2 meta next to ln q (α = 0 is the market), the pooled α with a
  meeting-clustered 95% CI and the paired out-of-fold ΔLL against the market-only stage-2 meta (D7 bounds).
  Pre-registered grid: drift_var_per_day {0, 0.004} × t_df {normal, 5}, both methods.
- **Result (#347):** no gain over the final price, so E39 does not reject the null.
  - Over D4's window (normal error, 22,088 marginal races, 2020-Q2 to 2026-11-23), the stage-2 meta with p_speed
    against the market-only meta gives ΔLL −0.00018 to +0.00003 nats per race. No lower bound is above 0, and the
    largest one-sided upper bound is +0.00021.
  - The pooled α is −0.031 to −0.006; where its CI excludes 0 (drift 0.004) it is negative.
  - Alone, p_speed is 0.44 to 0.47 nats per race behind D4's calibrated market (pseudo-R² 0.08 to 0.09 against
    0.26; 22,968 races).
  - Sixteen variants, none failed ([docs/experiments/p-speed-japan.md](../../../../docs/experiments/p-speed-japan.md)).
- **Limitations:**
  - A Student-t fit takes about 15 minutes per fold at 788k training runs, so the Student-t points cover 2026-Q2 to
    Q4 only; the full-window rerun is #651.
  - The publication proxy (post + 30 min) and the race-level limitations above (rail, layout) carry over.

### C4: meta-model (#87; leaderboard input)

- C4 meta-model (#87, `models/meta.py` over `models/condlogit.py`): a conditional logit within race at the final Win price. Stage 2 is u_i = Σ_k α_k(z) m_ki + β ln q_i. The offset form is u_i = ln q_i + Σ_k α_k(z) m_ki, where the offset can be ln q or the D4 calibrated market. α and β are unbounded maximum likelihood (08 R13), and dead heats count at 1/k. An optional ridge shrinks only the member weights, toward the owner's target α = 0 (the market alone). Segment weights enter as interaction columns, α_k(z) = α_k0 + Σ_j α_kj z_j, and an optional (ln q)² term is available. The members m_k are out-of-fold level-1 log-probabilities. The meta is itself out of fold on D9's folds (`oof_predictions`, which refuses leaky or overlapping folds), and `predict(frame, q)` takes any price vector, so the decision layer can pass nowcast draws. `ceiling_table` gives E9's ln(π/q) table. Coefficient standard errors are the inverse information (races independent) and, given a cluster column, the meeting-clustered sandwich that D7's unit calls for. It feeds the leaderboard. Its first run (`studies/meta.py`, `docs/experiments/meta-japan.md`) used HKTrack's research-24 members. These read JRDB, or are not confirmed free of it, so their results stay under the data dir. In that run the meta trains on member rows from 2023-01-05 only, so D9's 5-season minimum is not met at level 2 on any scored fold (0.98 to 3.73 years), and level 1 is HKTrack's season walk-forward, refit once a season, not quarterly. By the owner's answer (b) on #87 (2026-11-25), that run is a documented failure to meet D9, and the D9-compliant run comes with #91's and #98's members.

### C4: the meld of the members (#98; ranked, not adopted)

- C4 meld (#98, `models/meld.py` over `models/meta.py`): one stage-2 conditional logit at the final Win price, u_i = Σ_k α_k(z) w_ik m_ik + β ln q_i, the members m_k out-of-fold log-probabilities. `member_frame` aligns each member by race and horse and refuses a row whose `oof_fold` is not the meld's own D9 quarter for it (`stacking.InvalidProvenance`); a race with a runner lacking a member is dropped whole. Gates give every gate cell (the tuple of the row's gate values) its own α_k(z), segment slopes as in #87; the market terms are shared and never penalised, and a ridge shrinks every member term toward α = 0 (the owner's target). E41's precision weight is w_ik = 1 / (1 + κ_k σ_ik²), so a runner's member term is α_k0 / (1 + κ_k σ_ik²). `MeldFit.predict(frame, q)` reads the raw columns and takes any price vector; a gate cell the fit never saw predicts with its member terms at 0 (the market alone). `walk_forward_predictions` scores each quarter by fits on every earlier row at least D9's embargo before it, and `ablation_table` gives the marginal table (all against all minus k) on one set of meeting draws. `ambiguity` is Heskes' term (the mean KL of the members' normalised geometric mean to each member) and `member_correlations` the within-race correlations.
- Its run (`studies/meld.py`, `python -m horsetrack.countries.japan.studies.meld --run`, `docs/experiments/meld-japan.md`; family `japan-c4-meld-final-win`): #91's rating (σ = sigma_before), #92's ability model (σ = se_u; JRA-VAN's sire) and #93's six interaction members, in two populations: every member from 2020-Q1, and every member but the rating from 2015-Q1. Each population's quarters before the first with 5 seasons of level-2 training only train; every later quarter is scored. Fits are stage 2 with ridge 1 on the member terms; the comparator is the market-only stage-2 meta. Variants: each member standalone, the meld of all and of all minus each member, the marginal gains, encompassing, E41 (each σ member at three κ, with Clark-West against the constant-α meld), E42 (gates on debut races, handicaps and both), correlations and ambiguity, each model's N5 top claim band at the recorded Win dividends as its leaderboard row. #94's competition terms, #95's lineage members, #97's p_speed and E41's R_eff have no out-of-fold export and are failed records. A record with the ability member is licensed (D21).

### C6: shadow decision layer (#100; leaderboard input)

- C6 shadow decider (#100, `models/decision.py`): the EV per yen of a flat stake on every combination of the Win, Place and Wide boards at t, as the mean over C1's nowcast draws of P × D, never P̄ × D̄ (08 R5). P comes from C4's P(win) on Win draw m, and, for Place and Wide, C5's ordered top three from it; D is the dividend in draw m of the pool's own draws with our stake inside it, the money on a combination being the draw's share × V_final with each read as the decimal it prints as, broken to 10 yen, at least 110 and 100 when the Plus 10 is withheld. Place and Wide enumerate every ordered top three and pay the ticket by the stake-back-share rule with its co-placers; Place pays the places of the field at sale (`decide(view, field_at_sale=)`, by default the Win board's runners). The cross-pool coupling is an assumption: Win and Place/Wide draws share per-runner shocks (one iid N(0, 1) matrix per race and τ from a stable hash, handed to C1's `Nowcast.draws_from_shocks` for every pool, a Wide pair's shock (ε_a + ε_b)/√2), so the EV keeps the covariance of P and D within a draw; no other cross-pool dependence is modelled. One EV per refit of C4/C5 is reported beside each row. The decider calls `meta[r].predict(q, view)`, which C4's `MetaFit.predict(frame, q)` does not match: it needs an adapter, which no ticket has built yet (#101 logs and scores the decider's records but does not run it). `ShadowDecider` returns only `ShadowDecision` records (race, pool, combination, τ, the board at t, the EV per refit and config/betting.yaml's flat stake, within the race-day cap); it selects nothing, applies no threshold, refuses any mode but "shadow", and has no code path that places a bet. It has run on synthetic tests only. It feeds the leaderboard.
- C6 shadow scoring (#101, `models/shadow.py`; run `studies/shadow_run.py`, family `japan-shadow-calibration-v1`, spec `docs/specs/shadow-calibration-prereg.md`): `log_decisions` appends one record per `ShadowDecision` to the v0 shadow log through `ShadowLog.append` (its key, the board's figure at t, the claimed EV per yen over the refits; no stake key). `settle_shadow` settles every record as a ¥100 paper ticket through #454's `settle_bets` (won at its dividend, lost, refunded on a scratch, unsettled, no_board; a holdout record stays 'holdout' and is never read). The calibration slope is the OLS slope, with an intercept, of the realised net return per yen at the final dividend on the claimed net EV, its bounds from a bootstrap of whole meetings (D7); it is reported, not a gate. `flat_stakes` gives the flat stake in order of decision time and rank until the ¥30,000 race-day cap, `log_growth_by_day` is ln(ending / starting) of the day's money, and `selection_drift` (E20) regresses the fall ln O_t − ln O_final on the claimed EV of the record `lag` minutes earlier. `read_bets` and `played_by_day` give the owner's hand-played bets by day and option beside each option's leaderboard rank. Nothing is placed, sized beyond the flat stake, or adopted.

## Known limitations

- C1 nowcast (#85, #410): Place is read by JRA's stake-back-share rule. v5's measurement (record `86795d4a880bbd20`) gives, on the closing boards of the scored Place cases, by read used and P, the share of validated boards whose figures the rule reproduces from the votes and the share whose shares fall within 7.5% relative and 0.01 absolute per runner of the vote shares. Which reads stand as a result, and the rates, are withheld (D21) and are in the private report; the card does not assert the tolerance on every board. v5 read P from the priced runners; the reads and #116's drift baseline now take it from the field at the start of sales, a changed spec frozen as family `japan-c1-nowcast-final-shares-v6` (v5's freeze and records kept as history), and no run under that rule is made yet; `ShadowNowcast`'s draws still take P from the priced runners. Boards whose figures do not determine the money are read `undetermined` and are a diagnostic whatever their measurement. Those boards, and the boards the rule does not reproduce, are open follow-ups. The low figures alone fail where the favourite is at the floor on the tests' synthetic boards and are only the fallback for boards without high figures. Wide's inversion uses JRA's pair rule (`wide_money`), unmeasured against Wide money. The JRA-VAN paths are 5-6 minutes apart, so the board at T-2 and T-1 can be minutes old; its age at t is measured in the private report. φ_t is a point forecast, with no V_final uncertainty in the draws. A `RaceBoard` carries no lag boards, so through `ShadowNowcast` the velocity features are 0.
- C2 ability (#92): σ_i is the inverse Hessian of the discounted objective, which is conservative when w2 or w3 is below 1. The sire is the JRA-VAN pedigree's current snapshot (`static`), and the jockey and trainer are the store's keys, so a jockey's or trainer's effect pools every ride or runner in the training window, with no drift in time. The only horse-level feature is #91's rating; card features (weight, draw, age, sex, layoff) are left to #93-#95 (owner, 2026-11-26).
- C2 interactions (#93): blocks of more than 5,000 parameters (horse × jockey, the per-horse aptitude) use block-diagonal posterior variances, which bias a block strongly coupled to a base entity toward τ = 0, so such a block must stay under that size (trainer × track does, at 3,799 cells). τ's bounds come from one fold (2026-Q4). The base has no sire (D21) and fixed discounts. The level-1 fits of v2 come from two revisions (`56d0669`, `b5a726c`) whose code differs in the fixed point's step and stopping rule. Both families' registry summaries are over the public-tree cap and are kept under the data dir until #330.
- C2 lineage (#95): the store's netkeiba `horse_pedigree` links the sire, dam and damsire by name only (its id columns are empty) and covers mainly the foals of 2018 on, so a lineage prior from it reaches few runs before 2020. The prior has no per-horse effect, so a horse's own runs inform its sire's and damsire's effects as if independent.

- C4 meld (#98): level 2 trains on every earlier row of its population, so it is expanding from 2015-Q1 or 2020-Q1, not from 2014; a member's `member_train_end` is derived from D9's quarter of its `oof_fold` (the member studies fit each quarter on every earlier row to its embargo), not read from its export. The rating's out-of-fold rows start in 2020-Q1, so the population with every member scores only from 2025-Q2. E42's alphas are in-sample on the population's rows (meeting-clustered); the gated meld's out-of-fold ΔLL is the test. The meld's `MeldFit` is not yet behind `meta_adapter.MetaAdapter`, which reads `spec.members` as frame columns.
